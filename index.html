About

A short bio.

Styling is problem-solving with fabric — reading a body, an occasion, and a budget, then building something that actually works.

Styling people isn't just what I do — it's what I genuinely love.

I completed my bachelor's in Fashion Styling and Image Design at Pearl Academy in New Delhi, and later moved to London for my master's in Fashion Business Management at the University of Westminster, because I wanted to understand not just how to style but why it works. Over the past four years, I've styled influencers for brand shoots, events, ads, and music videos — building looks that had to perform in front of a camera, an audience, and sometimes both at once. I've also had the privilege of working with Paris Fashion Week, where I assisted styling teams with 30+ runway looks during show preparation inside a high-pressure, luxury fashion environment, and supported garment organisation, fittings, and backstage coordination under tight deadlines. Every project I take on, big or small, gets the same care — because that's the only way I know how to work.
